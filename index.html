What is a GDPS?

A Geometry Dash Private Server (GDPS) is a community-run server that takes the place of the official one. It handles things like accounts, level uploads, ratings, comments and leaderboards, completely separate from the official servers.

Why play on one?

Private servers can have their own rate standards, community-made levels and features the official servers don't offer. Your progress and account live on that server only, so a GDPS is a fresh space to play in.

What is KynorPS?

KynorPS is a small, independent GDPS maintained by Kynorie. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by RobTop Games.
