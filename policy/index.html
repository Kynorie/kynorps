Privacy Policy

This page explains how privacy works for KynorPS: what this website does (and doesn't) do, and who is responsible for the game server itself.

The GDPS is run by GDPSFH

KynorPS is maintained by Kynorie, but the server itself (accounts, levels, saves and hosting) is provided by GDPSFH (also known as FHGDPS). Anything you send to the server, such as registering an account, is handled by GDPSFH under their own privacy policy. Please read it at fhgdps.com/privacy.php.

This website doesn't track you

This website does not:

  • use analytics or tracking scripts
  • set cookies or store anything in your browser (the embedded video is covered below)
  • ask for or collect personal information
  • have accounts, forms or comment sections

The Linux check on the Download page runs entirely in your browser. Nothing about your system is sent anywhere.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may process standard technical data (such as your IP address) to deliver pages and keep the service secure, under its own privacy policy. We don't use this data for tracking.

Embedded video

The Download page has a YouTube video tutorial. It uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), but YouTube may still receive technical data such as your IP address when the video loads, under Google's privacy policy.

Third-party services

KynorPS relies on the services below. Each one handles your data under its own privacy policy, not ours.

  • GDPSFH / FHGDPS (game server, accounts, levels and saves): fhgdps.com/privacy.php
  • MediaFire (PC and Android downloads): MediaFire Privacy Policy
  • YouTube / Google (video tutorial): Google Privacy Policy
  • Vercel (website hosting): Vercel Privacy Policy
  • Discord (community server and Rich Presence): Discord Privacy Policy
  • GitHub / Geode (Geode installer download; Geode's install script is served from geode-sdk.org): GitHub Privacy Statement
  • WineHQ (Wine, for Linux players): WineHQ Privacy Policy

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. KynorPS is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RobTop Games.
