KynorPS is a Geometry Dash Private Server (GDPS) maintained by Kynorie. It provides a custom Geometry Dash experience separate from the official servers, with its own identity, configuration, and community.

KynorPS is built around the idea of offering an alternative way to experience Geometry Dash, giving players a separate environment to explore, play, and enjoy the game. It operates independently of the official Geometry Dash servers and is maintained as a personal project by Kynorie.

Whether you're interested in playing custom content, exploring a different GDPS environment, or simply experiencing Geometry Dash outside its official infrastructure, KynorPS aims to provide a straightforward and unique experience.

KynorPS is an independent, community-oriented project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RobTop Games.

To create an account, first make sure you have the app installed. After that, go to Settings > Account > Help. You will then be greeted by the GDPSFH panel, where you should see a button that says "Create an account" / "Register on KynorPS". Click on that and fill out the information. Afterwards, try to log in to the account you created in the KynorPS app by going to Settings > Account > Login.
